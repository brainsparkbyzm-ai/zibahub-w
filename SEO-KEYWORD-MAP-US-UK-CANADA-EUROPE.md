## Country modifiers and language

Use country-specific pages only when the platform has enough relevant vacancies or genuinely useful local career information.

- **United States:** state, metro and city modifiers; apprentice / journeyman / master terminology; state licensing and BLS wage references.
- **Canada:** province and city modifiers; apprenticeship and Red Seal context; Job Bank wage data and provincial authority links.
- **United Kingdom:** town/region modifiers; apprenticeship, ECS/CSCS and recognised qualification context; official GOV.UK and CITB sources.
- **Europe:** country and region modifiers only where the site can provide local-language, work-eligibility and qualification context. Do not imply one universal European licence or wage standard.

## Internal-link rules

1. Every published trade profile links to matching live jobs, its salary page and relevant training/apprenticeship routes.
2. Job details link back to the canonical trade profile and relevant location/category hub where the relationship is genuine.
3. Salary pages link to their methodology and dated primary source, plus relevant trade and job pages.
4. Guides link to one or two directly relevant next steps, not every site section.
5. Employer pages link to active vacancies and the employer posting page.
6. Avoid multiple indexable pages competing for the same primary query. Assign one primary page per intent cluster.

## Indexation and content quality gates

- Do not index empty trade/country/location combinations.
- Do not generate pages whose only difference is a city or keyword swap.
- Do not index seed/sample vacancies or unverified employer profiles as real opportunities.
- Remove expired jobs from the sitemap; use a suitable expired-job response or redirect policy rather than leaving stale vacancies indexed.
- For short imported job descriptions, preserve the original facts and source; do not add fabricated requirements, salary, benefits, employer claims or location details to inflate word count.
- Where two vacancy records are the same opening, consolidate only after confirming source ID, employer, location and application URL. Similar titles alone are not enough to merge.
- Measure query impressions, clicks, CTR and position in Search Console before deciding which keyword pages to expand.
