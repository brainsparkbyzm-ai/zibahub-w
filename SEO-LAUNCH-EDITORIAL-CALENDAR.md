# ZibaHub SEO Launch Editorial Calendar

> Status: proposed 100-post backlog only. These are not published posts. Each article must be researched, written originally, fact-checked, internally linked and reviewed before publication.

## Editorial standards

- Answer one clear searcher's question with original explanations, examples, checklists, comparisons or calculations.
- Use dated primary sources for pay, labour outlook, licensing, apprenticeship rules and course recognition. State geography, currency, measurement unit and reference period.
- Do not copy job-board descriptions or competitor articles. Do not publish city/trade permutations without distinct local data.
- Show author/reviewer and last-checked date. Correct or retire outdated country-specific advice.
- Link to relevant ZibaHub jobs, trade profiles, apprenticeship pages, salaries, calculators and guides only when contextually useful.
- Label sponsored content and qualify paid links appropriately. Never promise a backlink in exchange for editorial acceptance.
