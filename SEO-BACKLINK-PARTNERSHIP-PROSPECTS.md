## Outreach sequence

1. Review each organisation's current external-link policy, editorial guidelines and audience. Do not submit generic link requests.
2. Lead with a useful asset: an original wage comparison with dated primary sources, a verified trade vacancy dataset, a clear licensing explainer, or a free employer vacancy pilot.
3. Personalise the email to the named programme/resource and ask whether the resource is appropriate for their audience. Do not demand a do-follow link.
4. Send one initial message and at most one polite follow-up after 7–10 business days. Respect opt-outs and do-not-contact requests.
5. Record organisation, public contact route, source URL, date checked, contact date, follow-up date, reply, and any resulting live URL.
6. Count a backlink only after the referring page is live and independently verified. Separate nofollow, sponsored, directory, editorial and partner links in reporting.

## Link-worthy assets ZibaHub should create first

- **Country-aware trade pay guide:** cite official wage data and disclose year, region, currency, hourly/annual basis and methodology.
- **Apprenticeship pathway directory:** link directly to official national/provincial/state sources; clearly label independent providers.
- **Trade hiring snapshot:** publish only after enough deduplicated, dated job data exists; explain coverage and limitations.
- **Employer vacancy toolkit:** practical, original hiring templates and role-description guidance for small trade contractors.
- **Licensing and certification explainer:** country-specific, reviewed against the actual regulator; never imply one licence applies everywhere.

## Qualification and reporting fields

For every outreach target, record:
- Official domain and exact resource page
- Country, trade and audience fit
- Publicly published contact route
- Whether external links are editorially accepted
- Proposed asset and why it benefits their audience
- First contact / follow-up / opt-out status
- Reply and collaboration status
- Resulting live URL and link attribute (if any)
